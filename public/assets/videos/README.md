# 3D Video Background

Place your 3D animated video here.

**File:** `3d-gaming-scene.mp4`

**Recommended specifications:**
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
- **Duration:** Loop-friendly (10-30 seconds)
- **File Size:** Optimize for web (under 10MB if possible)
- **Content:** 3D animated gaming pads and laptop scene

**Alternative formats supported:**
- WebM (better compression)
- OGG (fallback)

The video will automatically loop and play muted in the background.
